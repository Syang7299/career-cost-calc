Why the gap between cosmetology and the rest is so large

Cosmetology's cost is dominated by tuition paid upfront before you earn anything. Electrician and CDL both have low-or-no-tuition paths (apprenticeship, or a short intensive program), so their total cost is mostly fees and tools, not tuition. Real estate and personal trainer are the cheapest because neither requires a multi-week training program at all - just a course, an exam, and in real estate's case, insurance.

None of this means cosmetology is a worse choice - it means the payback math looks different, and that's exactly why a single "average cost of a career change" number is close to useless for actually deciding between options.

FAQ

What is the cheapest license to get?

Of the five careers compared here, personal trainer certification is the cheapest to get started (around $674 for a self-study package plus CPR/AED), followed by a real estate license (around $1,076 in fees). Electrician and CDL cost more upfront (roughly $4,350 and $6,400) but pay back fast because they lead to higher wages. Cosmetology has the highest upfront cost at roughly $16,575.

Which license pays back the fastest?

Using each calculator's default assumptions, personal trainer and real estate both pay back in about 1 month, electrician and CDL in about 2 months, and cosmetology in about 8 months - but payback time depends heavily on your own current income, so these are starting points, not guarantees.
