Why these numbers, not someone else's average

Most "how much does it cost" articles give you one national average and stop there. These calculators use your actual numbers - your school's quote, your state's fees, your expected income - because tuition alone can range from $5,000 to $20,000 depending on the program, and a national average won't tell you when you break even.

Every calculator on this site is free, runs entirely in your browser, and doesn't ask you to sign up.

CareerCost is an independent tool and is not affiliated with any school, licensing board, or certifying body. Figures are general ranges - confirm exact costs and requirements with your chosen school and state board before enrolling.
